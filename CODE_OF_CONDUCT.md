# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want CatalogForge AI to be a respectful, constructive, and welcoming project for contributors of all experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
- Give actionable feedback without personal attacks.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on ideas, tradeoffs, and evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Disruptive behavior that makes collaboration unsafe or unproductive.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this code of conduct.
